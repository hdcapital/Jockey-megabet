BONUS BACK PICKER - which horse to back when Sportsbet offers
"money back as a bonus bet if your horse runs 2nd or 3rd"
==================================================================

ONE-TIME SETUP
  1. Install Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/
     (when installing, TICK "Add Python to PATH").
  2. Unzip this folder somewhere easy, e.g. your Desktop.

EVERY TIME
  Double-click  "BONUS BACK.bat"

  It asks three things. Type a number and press Enter:
    1. How much you're betting   (Enter = same as last time, starts at $50)
    2. Which meeting             (a numbered list of today's meetings)
    3. Which race                (a numbered list - or just press Enter for
                                  every race left at that meeting)

  It then shows, in a green box, the horse to back and roughly what the
  bet is worth to you. A page with every horse's numbers opens in your
  browser too. Press Enter to do another race, or type Q to quit.

  "NO BET" in a yellow box means no horse in that race is worth it.

TIPS
  - Check the race again in the 10 minutes before it jumps; prices move.
  - Optional: put your Betfair login in a file called .env (copy
    .env.example) for slightly sharper chances. It works fine without.
  - It never places a bet. You place the bet on Sportsbet yourself.

(For the place-betting scanner that also lives in this folder, see
