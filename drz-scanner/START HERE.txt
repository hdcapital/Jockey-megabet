BONUS BACK PICKER - which horse to back when Sportsbet offers
"money back as a bonus bet if your horse runs 2nd or 3rd"
==================================================================

ONE-TIME SETUP
  1. Install Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/
     (when installing, TICK "Add Python to PATH").
  2. Unzip this folder somewhere easy, e.g. your Desktop.

EVERY TIME
  Double-click  "BONUS BACK.bat"

  First it asks what you want to do. Type a number and press Enter:

  1 = PICK A HORSE FOR THE PROMO
    It asks how much you're betting (Enter = same as last time), then the
    meeting and race from numbered lists. A green box shows the horse to
    back and roughly what the bet is worth. A page with every horse's
    numbers opens in your browser too.
    "NO BET" in a yellow box means no horse in that race is worth it.

  2 = USE A BONUS BET I'VE GOT
    It asks how big the bonus bet is, then where you want to use it
    (or press Enter for every race in the next hour). It tells you
    exactly what to do:
      - with Betfair: which horse to put the bonus bet on at Sportsbet,
        and the exact LAY to place on Betfair, so you keep the same cash
        whatever happens (usually 70-80% of the bonus).
      - without Betfair: the best horse to just bet it on.

  Press Enter to go again, M for the main menu, or Q to quit.

  The bonus bet's value is worked out from today's real prices each time,
  and the screen tells you which horse and price that came from.

TIPS
  - Check the race again in the 10 minutes before it jumps; prices move.
  - Optional: put your Betfair login in a file called .env (copy
    .env.example) for slightly sharper chances. It works fine without.
  - It never places a bet. You place the bet on Sportsbet yourself.
